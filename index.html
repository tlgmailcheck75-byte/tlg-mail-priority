TLG Mail Priority

A personal Android app that helps organise Gmail for TLG accounting work.

What it does

  • Reads the user's own Gmail with read-only access, after the user signs in with Google.
  • Sorts each email thread into accounting categories (invoices, Bill.com, AR, reconciliation, payroll and others).
  • Assigns a High, Medium or Low priority and writes a short summary and a suggested action.
  • Plans the work on a Monday–Saturday schedule and tracks it until the user marks it done.

What it never does

  • It never sends, replies to, forwards, deletes or changes any email.
  • It never sends email content to any server, AI service or third party. All processing happens on the phone.
