TLG Mail Priority

A private Android app and website that sort Gmail and Outlook by what needs doing first, for TLG accounting work.

What it does

  • Reads the user's own mailbox, read-only, after they sign in with Google or Microsoft.
  • Sorts each email thread into accounting categories (invoices, Bill.com, AR, reconciliation, payroll and others).
  • Assigns a High, Medium or Low priority and writes a short summary and a suggested action.
  • Shows what is overdue, due today and still waiting, until the user marks it done.
  • Keeps the phone and the website in step, so the same work shows up in both.

What it never does

  • It never sends, replies to, forwards, deletes or changes any email. Every mail permission it asks for is read-only.
  • It never uploads email content. Mail is read by your own phone or browser and stays there.
  • It carries no analytics, trackers or advertising, and nothing is used to train any AI model.
